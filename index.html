Privacy Policy

VerseWalls · com.sathwik.versewalls · Last updated 3 August 2026

VerseWalls has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the VerseWalls Android application (package com.sathwik.versewalls), published by Sathwik Samuel. It is the only product this policy covers.

Information the app collects

None. VerseWalls does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device information. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, or any identifier, and it has no way to send data even if it did.

The app is built without the INTERNET permission. Android therefore blocks it from making any network connection at all — this is enforced by the operating system, not merely by our policy.

Information the app stores on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It stays on your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Your settings — chosen language, wallpaper style, pinned verse, schedule time, text position, target screen, and starred styles.
  • Verses you add yourself, if you use the "Add your own verse" feature.
  • A short history of recently applied designs. This stores only references — the style name, verse reference, and a number used to redraw it. No images are saved.

Uninstalling VerseWalls deletes all of it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no account to close.

Android backup

VerseWalls allows Android's standard backup feature. If you have backup switched on in your device settings, your app settings may be included in your phone's backup to your own Google account, the same as for other apps on your device. That transfer is performed by Android, not by VerseWalls, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can disable backup in your device's system settings.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Set wallpaper — to change your wallpaper, which is the entire purpose of the app.
  • Post notifications (optional) — only used if you switch on the morning verse notification. Declining it leaves every other feature working normally.
  • Write to storage (Android 9 and older only) — used only at the moment you tap Save, to write a wallpaper into your gallery. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

VerseWalls requests no other permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, call logs, messages, or files you have not explicitly asked it to save.

Saving and sharing wallpapers

When you tap Save, the image is written to your device's gallery. When you tap Share, Android hands the image to whichever app you pick. From that point the image is in that app's hands, and what happens to it is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Third parties

VerseWalls contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking libraries of any kind. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Children

VerseWalls is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no content shared between users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
