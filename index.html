Privacy Policy

VerseWalls · com.sathwik.versewalls · Last updated 9 October 2026

VerseWalls has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the VerseWalls Android application (package com.sathwik.versewalls), published by Sathwik Samuel. It is the only product this policy covers.

Information the app collects

None. VerseWalls does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device information. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, or any identifier, and it has no way to send data even if it did.

The app is built without the INTERNET permission. Android therefore blocks it from making any network connection at all — this is enforced by the operating system, not merely by our policy. That stays true with in-app purchases and update checks: both are carried out by the Google Play Store app on your phone, which VerseWalls asks; VerseWalls itself still cannot go online.

Information the app stores on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It stays on your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Your settings — chosen language, wallpaper style, pinned verse, schedule time, text position, target screen, and starred styles.
  • Verses you add yourself, if you use the "Add your own verse" feature.
  • A short history of recently applied designs. This stores only references — the style name, verse reference, and a number used to redraw it. No images are saved.
  • Whether you own the VerseWalls Collection, and its price as Google Play last reported it, so the Collection works offline.

Uninstalling VerseWalls deletes all of it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no account to close.

Purchases

The VerseWalls Collection is an optional one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Payment happens entirely in Google Play: VerseWalls never sees your card, UPI or account details. What the app receives, from the Play Store app on your phone, is confirmation of what you own — the product and a purchase token — which it checks on your device and remembers so the Collection works offline. None of it is sent to us; there is no server to send it to. Google Play's handling of your payment is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Updates

The app may show that a newer version is waiting. It learns this by asking the Play Store app on your phone; the Play Store does any network work, under Google's privacy policy.

Android backup

VerseWalls allows Android's standard backup feature. If you have backup switched on in your device settings, your app settings may be included in your phone's backup to your own Google account, the same as for other apps on your device. That transfer is performed by Android, not by VerseWalls, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can disable backup in your device's system settings.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Set wallpaper and set wallpaper hints — to change your wallpaper, which is the entire purpose of the app, and to tell your launcher it is one screen wide.
  • Post notifications (optional) — only used if you switch on the morning verse notification, or to say an app update is waiting. Declining it leaves every other feature working normally.
  • Schedule exact alarms and run at startup — so the daily change happens at the time you set, and is restored after your phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing — to buy the VerseWalls Collection through Google Play.
  • Wake lock, foreground service and network state — added by Android's own scheduling library (WorkManager), which uses them to run the daily change reliably. Network state only tells it whether the phone is online; the app still has no permission to go online itself.
  • Write to storage (Android 9 and older only) — used only at the moment you tap Save, to write a wallpaper into your gallery. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

VerseWalls requests no other permissions, and never the INTERNET permission. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, call logs, messages, or files you have not explicitly asked it to save.

Saving and sharing wallpapers

When you tap Save, the image is written to your device's gallery. Unless you own the VerseWalls Collection, saved and shared images carry a small VerseWalls mark; the wallpaper on your own screen never does. When you tap Share, Android hands the image to whichever app you pick. From that point the image is in that app's hands, and what happens to it is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Third parties

VerseWalls contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking libraries of any kind. Its only Google libraries are Google Play's billing and in-app update libraries, which talk to the Play Store app on your phone. Google's billing library includes a component that would upload the library's own diagnostics; VerseWalls removes its internet permission, so nothing it records leaves your phone. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Children

VerseWalls is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no advertising and no content shared between users. It offers one optional in-app purchase, made through Google Play, which is subject to Google Play's own controls, including parental purchase approval.

Changes to this policy
